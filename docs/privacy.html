Inkwell

Privacy policy. Last updated 18 October 2026.

The short version

Inkwell does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not send anything to servers run by the developer. Your notes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

Your notes

Notebooks, handwriting and imported PDFs are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own iCloud Drive and synced by Apple between the devices signed in to your Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access them. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Calendar

If you allow it, Inkwell reads your calendar on the device to suggest notebooks for your lectures and events. Calendar data is never copied off your device or sent anywhere.

Folders and files you choose

If you link a folder or import a PDF, Inkwell reads only the files you pick, on your device, to keep your notebooks up to date.

Sharing pages with Claude on your Mac (optional)

This feature is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, Inkwell saves images of your pages in your own iCloud Drive, and sends them directly to your own Mac over your local Wi-Fi network, so that the Inkwell plugin on that Mac can show them to Claude. The pages go from your device to your Mac; Inkwell does not send them to any other server. What happens after you share a page with Claude on your Mac is covered by the privacy policy of the Claude app you use there.

Children

Inkwell does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
