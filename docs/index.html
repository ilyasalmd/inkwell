Inkwell

A free, open source handwriting notes app for iPad and Mac, built around Apple Pencil. Folders like Finder, notebooks you can design, and schedules that make your weekly notebooks for you.

Install on iPad

iPadOS only runs apps signed by an Apple ID, so a sideloading tool signs Inkwell with yours. It is free.

  1. Install AltStore or SideStore on your iPad. Follow the steps on altstore.io or sidestore.io.
  2. On the iPad, tap Add to AltStore above, or open Inkwell.ipa from Files and choose Share, then AltStore.
  3. Open Inkwell from the Home Screen. If iPadOS asks, go to Settings, General, VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID.

With a free Apple ID, apps stop opening after 7 days. AltStore renews them automatically while it can reach your Mac or Wi-Fi. With a paid Apple Developer account the limit is a year.

Install on Mac

  1. Download Inkwell.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Inkwell onto the Applications folder.
  3. If macOS says it can't verify the app, right-click Inkwell in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. This happens only until a release has been notarized by Apple.

What it does

  • Apple Pencil Pro squeeze and double-tap, pressure and tilt
  • Pens, highlighters, colours and a quick palette
  • Cornell, squared, lined and dotted pages on any paper colour
  • Notebook cover designer, including drawing your own cover
  • Finder-style folders you can colour and name
  • Schedules that create folders or notebooks every week
  • Calendar-linked suggestions and optional reminders
  • Import PDFs, including Goodnotes exports, and link a folder of PDFs
  • Light, dark and colour themes

Good to know

  • iCloud sync is not in the open download, because it needs a paid developer team. Build from source with your own team to switch it on.
  • Goodnotes' own .goodnotes files can't be read. Export a notebook from Goodnotes as PDF and import that.
  • Updates are manual: download the new release, or add the AltStore source and AltStore will tell you.
