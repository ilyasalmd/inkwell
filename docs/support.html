Support. Handwriting notes for iPad and Mac, built around Apple Pencil.

Get help

Email ilyasdaneshmand@gmail.com with what you tapped and what happened, and a screenshot if you can. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

  1. How do I sync between iPad and Mac? Turn on iCloud sync in Inkwell’s Settings on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account.
  2. I deleted a notebook by mistake. Tap the bin at the bottom right of the Library to open the Trash, then choose Put Back.
  3. Can I bring notes from another app? Export them as PDF, then share the PDF to Inkwell or import it with the + button. Your writing goes on its own layer above each page.
  4. Where are backups? Inkwell keeps automatic backups of your library and can restore one as a copy from Settings.
